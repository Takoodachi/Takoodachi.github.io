Price comparison + trackingOct 2025 – Sep 2026

PriceTrack

Search once, compare prices at official stores like Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart and Target, and track favorites on a dashboard that re-checks prices in the background.

  • Queries every store in parallel, answers within 2.5 s and streams in slow sources as they arrive.
  • Matches one product across stores by UPC, model number and title similarity, conservatively.
  • Redis cache with single-flight requests; a BullMQ worker runs the scheduled re-checks.
